Iowa | Chicago | Kansas City | Omaha | St Louis | Minneapolis | Puerto Vallarta | Cabo San Lucas & More

Social Call is a live cover band specializing in weddings, corporate events, private parties, galas, festivals, and special events. Our versatile repertoire combines Pop, R&B, Soul/Motown, Funk, Rock, Hip-Hop, Dance, and Jazz, delivering an energetic live-music experience tailored to each event. Formed in Des Moines, Social Call is available as a customizable 3 to 14 piece band for events throughout Iowa and the Midwest.

Weddings

Let us help you get your wedding crew on the dance floor. We can provide music for the Ceremony, Cocktail Hour, and Reception. The cocktail hour could begin with a small jazz trio and pick up later on with top-notch performances of your favorite party classics.

Corporate Events

World class service for corporate events. We tailor the songs to the vibe you want to set and bring a professional image too that's sure to impress. We cover Rock, Pop, Funk/Motown, Dance, Jazz, Holiday, and More.

House Parties

We love house parties and it's how we got our start. With different sizes and configurations of the band available, we have options to suit your budget we'll play your favorite songs and give your attendees an unforgettable experience.
